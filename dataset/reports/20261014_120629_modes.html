Notes
Passive 100G to 4x25G Ethernet Direct Attach Cable (DAC) coax solution; not an optical transceiver. Cable lengths 1 to 5 m (3.3 to 16.4 ft). Break-out from QSFP28 side is direct for 2 m and below; for 3 m and above, break-out from QSFP28 is at 1 m. AWG30 for 1m/2m/3m variants per part table; AWG26 for 5m (PCU5M). Operating case temperature 0 to +70 C. Protocol agnostic support stated as 1000GbE and EDR InfiniBand.
