Optical Transceivers ## FTLF8538P5BCZ ## **25GBASE-SR Low Latency SFP+ Transceiver** FTLF8538P5BCz SFP+ transceivers are designed for use in 25Gb/s data rate over multimode fiber. They are compliant to IEEE802.3by, SFF-8472 Rev 12.2band SFF-8402g, and compatible with SFF-8432aand applicable portions of SFF-8431 Rev. 4.1c. The transceiver is RoHS compliant as described in Application Note AN-2038. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Up to 25.78 Gb/s bi-directional data links - Hot-pluggable SFP+ footprint - Built-in digital diagnostic functions - 25GE SR - 100G SR4 fan out to 4x25GE SR - eCPRI - 850nm Oxide VCSEL laser transmitter - Duplex LC connector - RoHS compliant - 100m over M5F MMF (50/125 um OM4) - 70m over M5E MMF (50/125um OM3) - 1E-12 BER over 30m OM3 / 40m OM4 MMF - Metal enclosure, for lower EMI - 1.0W maximum power consumption - Single 3.3V power supply - Operating temperature range: 0°C to 70°C **FTLF8538P5BCz** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLF8538P5BCz** z=L: Single rate (25GbE only) z=V: Dual rate (25GbE and 10GbE) ## **I.
