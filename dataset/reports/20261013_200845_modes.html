Recommended case temperature 0°C to 70°C (Table 5). Supply VccT/VccR 3.135–3.465 V; max module supply current 300 mA (Table 6, Pin Description Note 6). Receiver sensitivity is specified as Input Optical Modulation Amplitude (OMA) in µW (89 µW @ 14.025 Gb/s, 76 µW @ 8.5 Gb/s, 61 µW @ 4.25 Gb/s unstressed; stressed OMA 170/151/148–126 µW per rate/fiber), not as rx_sensitivity_dbm. Average optical output power minimum −7.8 dBm; maximum Pout is the lesser of Class 1 safety limits or received power max (Table 8 Note 1). Link distances from EEPROM Table 12 notes: at 14.025 Gb/s—100 m OM3, 35 m OM2, 15 m OM1; at 8.5 Gb/s—150 m OM3, 50 m OM2, 25 m OM1; at 4.25 Gb/s—380 m OM3, 150 m OM2, 70 m OM1. Tx/Rx CDR engaged at RS high (14.025 Gb/s); bypassed at 8.5/4.25 Gb/s. Pout when Tx_DISABLE asserted ≤ −35 dBm.
