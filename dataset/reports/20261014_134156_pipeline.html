DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-SFP-10GE-LR10-Cxx** SFP+, 10G Multirate, CWDM, DDM, 10dB, 10km, 1270nm-1610nm (18ch) ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-10GE-LR10-Cxx is a versatile CWDM transceiver supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The distance performance is in accordance with the IEEE 802.3ae LR/LW-standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 10km for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-LR) and 10GbE-WAN (10GBASE-LW) services. The transceiver is available in 18 CWDM wavelength versions, spanning from 1270nm to 1610nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard.
