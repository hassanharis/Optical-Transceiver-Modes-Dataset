Notes
QSFP-DD type 2a. Nominal wavelengths stated as 191.275–196.125 THz tunable with 6.25 GHz grid; title references D9128–D9612. Operating temperature +15°C to +75°C; module may turn up from cold start at -5°C and reach spec after self-heating. Rx sens @ OSNR >36 dB. Rx @ OSNR column per footnote 2: [Min OSNR Value @ Min Rx power for the OSNR value]. Default vs maximum CDC range differ per appl mode; max CDC is provisionable and increases power consumption. Tx power ranges per mode; host may attenuate up to 10 dB from max available. Absolute max Rx signal +13 dBm, Rx total +15 dBm. Storage -40°C to +85°C. Enhanced mode1 not fully interoperable with OpenZR+. Latency: 400G CFEC 8 us, 400G oFEC 5 us, 300G oFEC 6 us, 200G oFEC 7 us, 100G oFEC 11 us.
