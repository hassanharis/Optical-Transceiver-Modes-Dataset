LINK-PP INT’L TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED LQ-M85200-SR4C Rev1.0- Sep.2022 ## **LQ-M85200-SR4C** ## 200GBASE-SR4 QSFP56, Optical Transceiver Module, 100mReach ## **Features** - Hot-pluggable QSFP56 form factor - Built-in 200G PAM4 DSP - Supports 212.5Gb/saggregate bit rates - Supports 103.125Gb/s aggregate bit rates if required - Low power dissipation <5W - RoHS-6 compliant (lead-free) - Commercial case temperature rangeof 0°C to 70°C - Single 3.3V power supply - Maximum link length of 70m on OM3 MMF and 100m on OM4 & OM5 MMF - Uncooled 4 channels 850nm VCSEL array - 4 channels PIN photo detector array - 200GAUI-4 electrical interface - Single MPO12 receptacle - CMIS V4.0 compliant - Built-in digital diagnostic functionality ## **Applications** - IEEE 802.3cd 200GBASE-SR4 Ethernet(PAM4) ## **Description** LINK-PP LQ-M85200-SR4C 200GE QSFP56 Optical Transceiver modules are designed for use in 200 Gigabit Ethernet links over OM3/OM4/OM5 multi-mode fiber. They are compliant with the QSFP MSA and with IEEE 802.3cd 200GBASE-SR4 specification. Digital diagnostics functions are available via the I2C interface as specified by CMIS V4.0.
