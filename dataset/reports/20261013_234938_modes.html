Notes
Copper (electrical) SFP over Cat 5 UTP; not an optical transceiver. Datasheet title LP-SFP-MSRI: 1000 Mbps only, SERDES interface, -40~85°C (Industrial). Features also list Commercial 0 to +70°C for other variants. Electrical input: document states LP-SFP-xxxx input voltage +5V +/- 5%; Table 1 specifies +3.3V supply (Vcc 3.13–3.47 V). Caution: power consumption and surge current higher than SFP MSA. Reach stated as 100 m (Table 5, Description) and 0.1 Km (ordering); BER <10^-12 at 100 m Cat 5. Symbol rate 250 Mbps on each of four pairs. Line frequency 125 MHz (Table 3). LOS pin reflects link/receiver status per standard, not optical power in dBm.
