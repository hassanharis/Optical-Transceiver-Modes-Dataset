LINK-PP INT’L TECHNOLOGY CO., LIMITED LG-SC341G-20IMB1 Rev1.0- Sep.2016 ## **LG-SC341G-20IMB1** ## SFP SC 20km GPON ONU Stick with Mac 1.244Gbps 1310nm TX / 2.488Gbps 1490nm RX ## **Features** - SFP transceiver with integrated PON ONU MAC and SC/UPC interface, plug-and-play supported, enabling rapid deployment via automatic discovery and configuration - Adopts 1310nm DFB burst-mode transmitter and 1490nm TIA continuous-mode receiver - Single 3.3V power supply - Supports Digital Diagnostic Monitoring Interface (DDM) compliant with SFF-8472 standard - Compliant with SFP MSA (Multi-Source Agreement) standard - Low Electromagnetic Interference (EMI) design with excellent Electro-Static Discharge (ESD) protection performance - Compliant with IEC-60825 Class 1 laser safety standard - Compliant with RoHS environmental standard - XPON Stick optical transceiver compatible with both GPON and EPON systems - Compliant with SFP Multi-Source Agreement (MSA) SFF-8074i standard - Compliant with ITU-T G.984.2 and its Amendment 1 standard - Compliant with ITU-T G.988 ONU Management and Control Interface (OMCI) specification - Compliant with IEEE 802.3AH standard - Supports SGMII and 1000BASE-X interface protocols - Supports application scenarios of HGU (Home Gateway Unit) and SFU (Single Family Unit) - Operating temperature range:-40~+85℃ ## **Application** - Passive Optical Network (PON) ## **Description** This product is an SFP transceiver compliant with MSA standards. It integrates not only the optical components of an ONU, but also all required complete circuits. Known as a "PON Stick", it condenses a full FTTH ONU into an SFP module slightly larger than the standard form factor. It can be directly plugged into network devices such as switches and routers, enabling data ports to adapt to various fiber environments and transmission distances on demand.
