Notes
Copper 10GBase-T SFP+ module (RJ-45), not an optical transceiver. Cable reach stated as 30 m over Category 6a/7 UTP with BER <10^-12 (General Specifications). Supply current max 750 mA; input voltage 3.13–3.47 V (typ 3.3 V). Storage temperature -40 to 85 °C ambient. TX FAULT pin not supported. EEPROM at address A0h via 2-wire serial bus.
