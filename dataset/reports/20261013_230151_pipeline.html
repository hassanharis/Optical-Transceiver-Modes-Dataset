Optical Transceivers ## FTLF1429P3BCV ## **16GFC RoHS Compliant Long-Wavelength SFP+ Transceiver** FTLF1429P3BCV SFP+ transceivers are designed for use in Fibre Channel links up to 14.025 Gb/s data rate and up to 10 km link length. They are compliant with FC-PI-5 Rev. 6.101 and SFF-8472 Rev 11.03 and SFF-80817. They are compatible with SFF-8432[2] and applicable portions of SFF-8431 Rev. 4.14. The optical transceivers are compliant per the RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU. See Finisar Application Note AN-2038 for more details. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Up to 14.025 Gb/s bi-directionaldata links - Hot-pluggable SFP+ footprint - Built-in digital diagnostic functions - Tri Rate 4.25 / 8.5 / 14.025 Gb/sFibre Channel through Rate Select - 10GBASE-LR/LW 10G Ethernet - 1310nm DFB laser transmitter - Duplex LC connector - RoHS compliant and Lead Free - Up to 10 km on 9/125μm SMF - Metal enclosure, for lower EMI - 1W maximum power consumptionwith established link - Single 3.3V power supply - Extended operating temperaturerange: 0°C to 70°C **FTLF1429P3BCV** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLF1429P3BCV** ## **I.
