Optical Transceivers ## FTLC1155RGPLC ## **10km 100G eCWDM4 QSFP28 Optical Transceiver Module 4WDM-10** FTLC1155RGPLC QSFP28 transceiver modules are designed for use in 100 Gigabit Ethernet links on up to 10 km of single mode fiber (CWDM4 wavelength grid). Designed for outside plant applications with an extended temperature range (-20°C to +85°C), they are compliant with the QSFP28 MSA[1] , CWDM4 MSA[2] , 4WDM MSA[3] , and portions of IEEE P802.3bm CAUI4[4] . Digital diagnostic functions are available via the I2C interface, as specified by the QSFP28 MSA and Finisar Application Notes AN_x005F_xFFFE_2153[5] and AN-2154[6] . The optical transceiver is compliant per the RoHS-6 Directive 2011/65/EU[7] . See Finisar Application Note AN-2038[8 ] for more details. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Hot-pluggable QSFP28 form factor - Supports 103.1Gb/s aggregate bit rate - Power dissipation < 4.5W - 100G 4WDM applications with FEC - Outside plant - Reduced air flow central office - RoHS-6 compliant - Extended case temperature range of -20°C to +85°C - Adaptive CTLE - Single 3.3V power supply - Maximum link length of 10km onSingle Mode Fiber (SMF) [withKR4 FEC] - 4x25Gb/s CWDM transmitter - 4x25G retimed electrical interface - Duplex LC receptacles - I2C management interface **FTLC1155RGPLC** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLC1155RGPLC** 5: Gen 2 R: Ethernet maximum bit rate (103.1 Gb/s) G: 4x25G CWDM optical architecture P: Pull-tab release - L: LC straight receptacles C: Extended reach (10 km), extended temperature range ## **I.
