DATASHEET 5.2 ## **SO-SFP-10GE-LRM** ## SFP+, 10G Multirate, 1310nm MM, DDM, 3.5dB, 220m ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-10GE-LRM is a versatile 1310nm transceiver for MultiMode (MM) fiber supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The optical performance is in accordance with the IEEE 802.3ae LRM-standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 220m for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-LRM) services. The host system must have Electronic Dispersion Compensation (EDC) to fulfil the 10GBASE-LRM distance performance. The transceiver has no minimum distance (i.e. no minimum attenuation) which is ideal for intra-office connections since extra attenuators need not be considered.
