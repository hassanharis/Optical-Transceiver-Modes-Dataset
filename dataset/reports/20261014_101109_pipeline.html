1Mbps TTL SFP SMF 1310nm Optical Transceiver 丨 Data Sheet ## **OSP1-3120NxR** Low Rate 1Mb/s SFP Optical Transceiver Module, Single Mode, 1310nm, 20km Reach ## **Features** - ●Compliant with SFP MSA standard - ●Up to 1Mb/s data rate - ●Module integrates receive and transmit circuits of two parts - ●All-metal shell and the shell with plastic end plug form, LC receptacle. - ●Hot-pluggable - ●Single +3.3V power supply - ●Standard PECL data output and input with signal detect indication - ●High quality 1310nm VCSEL - ●ROHS compliant and Lead Free - ●Operating Temperature: Standard 0~70ºC Industrial -40~85ºC ## **Applications** - ●Industrial Ethernet Applications: industrial Ethernet Switches,Media Converters,Industrial fiber converters - ●Serial Communications over fiber: Industrial RS-232/RS-422/RS-485 serial to fiber media converter,industrial RS-232 to RS-422 and RS-485 Adapters - ●RS232/RS485/RS422 optical-electrical converter for electric power control, industrial control,industrial computing ## **Description** The OSP1-3120NxR family of SFP optical transceivers from Optcore provide the system designer and manufacturers with products to implement a range of industrial control, electric power control, special low data rate Ethernet designs at the 0-1Mb/s rate. To avoid the disadvantages of the traditional TTL 1x9 transceiver, this transceiver fully complies with the SFP Multi-Sourcing Agreement (MSA) and supports hot-pluggable. The 0~1M SFP transceiver is usually used for serial communications over fiber like RS-232/485 optical-electrical converter and industrial Ethernet networks to fiber networks communications like industrial fiber optic converters and industrial Ethernet switches. The 0~1M SFP optics are provided with commercial temperature grade and industrial temperature grade for different applications.
