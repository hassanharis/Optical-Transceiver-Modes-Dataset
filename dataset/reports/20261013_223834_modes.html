Notes
800G aggregate (2x400 DR4) OSFP Type 2; optional limited case temperature 20°C to +60°C vs commercial 0°C to +70°C. Reach 500 m on SMF G.652. Average launch power min -2.9 dBm and average receive power min -5.9 dBm are informative per IEEE 802.3bs notes. Receiver sensitivity (OMAouter) max -4.4 dBm defined for transmitter SECQ 0.9 dB; stressed receiver sensitivity (OMAouter) max -1.9 dBm per conformance test at TP3 for BER in 124.1.1 with SECQ 3.4 dB and aggressor OMAouter 4.2 dBm. Signaling rate per lane 53.125 ± 100 ppm. Module total power max 17 W across full temperature and voltage range. Regulatory section references FTCE4517E1PCA-2N (variant spelling). CMIS 4.0 per MSA.
