DATASHEET 5.0 ## **SO-SFP-1G-O-Cxx-E** SFP, OSC/OTDR, 1Gbps, CWDM, SM, 80km, LC, E-temp ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-1G-O-Cxx-E is an SFP form-factor transceiver for OSC (Optical Supervisory Channel) and OTDR (Optical Time Domain Reflectometer) applications. The transceiver operates as a OSC transceiver at 100Mbps and 1Gbps Ethernet rates up to 80km distances. Upon disruption of the data link, or failure to connect, the transmitter section of the SFP switches into OTDR mode. The SFPs measures the optical fiber link optical return loss during the OTDR start-up “handshaking” routine of the SFPs, before the data link is made. The receiver also measures the signal received. The SO-SFP-1G-O-Cxx-E is provided in three different wavelength versions according to the CWDM wavelength grid plus one at 1625nm. The transceiver is intended to be used together with the Smartoptics Open Line System products. ## TECHNICAL DATA |**Parameter**|**Value**||**Parameter**|**Value**| |---|---|---|---|---| |Technology|CWDM SFP||**Transmitter data:**|| |Transmission media|SM (2x LC)||Output power|Min: -2dBm1)| |Typical reach|80km|||Max: +1dBm1)| |Nominal wavelengths|1491nm3)||Transmit wavelengths|1484.5 – 1497.5nm3)| ||1511nm4)|||1504.5 – 1517.5nm4)| ||1611nm5)|||1604.5 – 1617.5nm5)| ||1625nm6)|||1618.5 – 1631.5nm6)| |Bit rate support|125Mbps / 1.25Gbps||**Receiver data:**|| |Protocol support|100M / 1G Ethernet||Minimum input power|-25dBm1) 2)| |Power budget|0 – 23dB||Overload (max power)|+1dBm1) 2)| |Power consumption|< 0.9W||Wavelength range|1260 – 1635nm| |Operating temperature|-20°C to +70°C||DDM|Yes| |Storage temperature|-40°C to +85°C||LOS Assert|Typ: -28dBm| ||||LOS De-Assert|Typ: -25dBm| |**OTDR data:**|||LOS Hysteresis|Min: 1dB| |Dynamic range|Typ: 65dB||MSA compliance|SFP MSA| |Dead zone|Max 30m|||| |Resolution|Max 10m||1)Average power|| |Accuracy|Max 50m||2)at 1.25Gbps PRBS7 and BER 1x10-12|| |3)SO-SFP-1G-O-C49-E|5)SO-SFP-1G-O-C61-E|||| |4)SO-SFP-1G-O-C51-E|6)SO-SFP-1G-O ...
