Notes
Product features state power dissipation < 1W; electrical note 1 states total power dissipation could exceed 1W when establishing link at case temperature below 25°C. Transmission distance: 100 m on OM4 or 70 m on OM3 (II. Recommended Operating Conditions). Data rates 8.5, 14.025, 28.05 Gbps correspond to 8G FC, 16G FC, and 32G FC applications. BER row lists 10^-12 and 10^-6 with notes: PRBS 2^7-1 for 8GFC, PRBS 2^31-1 for 16GFC, FEC for 32GFC; unstressed Rx sensitivity note 6 states for 32GFC with FEC, receiver sensitivity is at 10^-6 BER, not 10^-12. Transmitter average output power and OMA min values are rate-dependent (8.5, 14.025, 28.05 Gb/s). TWDP max 4.3 dB at 8.5 Gb/s; VECPQ max 2.56 dB at 14.025 Gb/s and 3.13 dB at 28.05 Gb/s. RIN max -129 dB/Hz at 28.05 Gb/s and -128 dB/Hz at 14.025 Gb/s and 8.5 Gb/s. Spectral width (RMS) max 0.57 nm at 28.05 Gb/s. Supply current Icc max 300 mA.
