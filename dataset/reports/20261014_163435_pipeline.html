DATASHEET 5.2 ## **SO-XFP-ER & -ER-I** XFP, 10G Multirate, 1550nm, SM, DDM, 16dB, 40km ## OVERVIEW The SO-XFP-ER is a versatile 1550nm XFP transceiver for SingleMode fiber supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The optical performance is in accordance with the IEEE 802.3ae standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 40km for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-ER) and 10GbE-WAN (10GBASE-EW) services. The support also spans down to 8.5 Gbps enabling the transceiver to be used for both 8G as well as 10G Fiberchannel services. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the INF-8077i specification.
