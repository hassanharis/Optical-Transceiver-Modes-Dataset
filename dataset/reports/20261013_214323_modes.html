TQ2020-BXXC-SO denotes the bi-directional pair solution; orderable variants are TQ2020-BADC-SO (Tx/Rx 1271/1331 nm) and TQ2020-BDAC-SO (Tx/Rx 1331/1271 nm). Single-fiber BiDi: two modules inject different wavelengths on one SM fiber. Module includes KP4 FEC; host system shall not have FEC activated.
