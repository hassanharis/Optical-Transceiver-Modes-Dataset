The output power of 0dBm unlocks the potential for the module to transmit 400G signals in already existing, 100GHz spacing, ROADM architectures. The OpenZR+ MSA provides a flexible solution for operators having routers that not yet have migrated to 400G services. The TQD014-TUNC-SO can as an example be used in the Smartoptics DCP-404 Muxponder to combine up to 4x 100G flows to a 100G/200G/300G/400G OpenZR+ signal to be transported over an optical network.
