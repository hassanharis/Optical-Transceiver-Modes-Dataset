Optical Transceivers ## FTLC9555SEPM ## **100m Multirate Parallel MMF 100G QSFP28 Optical Transceiver** FTLC9555SEPM 100G QSFP28 transceiver modules are designed for use in 100 Gigabit Ethernet, 128GFC and 4x28G OTN client links over multimode fiber. They are compliant with the QSFP28 MSA[1] , 128GFC2 and IEEE 802.3bm 100GBASESR4[3] and CAUI-4[3] . Digital diagnostics functions are available via the I2C interface, as specified by the QSFP28 MSA[1] and Finisar Application Note AN-2141[4] . The transceiver is RoHS-6 compliant and lead-free per Directive 2011/65/EU[5] , and Finisar Application Note AN-2038[6] . ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Hot-pluggable QSFP28 form factor - Supports 103.1Gb/s to 112.2Gb/s aggregate bit rates - Power dissipation < 2.5W - 100GBASE-SR4 100G Ethernet - 4x28Gb/s Multimode OTN - 128G Fibre Channel - RoHS-6 compliant - Commercial case temperature range of 0°C to 70°C - Single 3.3V power supply - Maximum link length of 100m on OM4 Multimode Fiber (MMF) - 4x25Gb/s 850mm VCSEL-based transmitter - 4x25G electrical interface - Single MPO12 receptacle - I2C management interface **FTLC9555SEPM** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLC9555SEPM** S: Multirate: maximum bit rate = 111.8 Gb/s E: 4x25G parallel optics P: Pull tab release M: MPO receptacle ## **I.
