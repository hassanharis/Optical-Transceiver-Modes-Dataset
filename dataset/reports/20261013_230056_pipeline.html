Optical Transceivers ## FTLF1429P3BCE ## **16GFC RoHS Compliant Long-Wavelength SFP+ Transceiver** FTLF1429P3BNV SFP+ transceivers are designed for use in Fibre Channel links up to 14.025 Gb/s data rate and up to 25 km link length. They are compliant with FC-PI-5 Rev. 6.101 and SFF-8472 Rev 11.03, and compatible with SFF-8432[2] and applicable portions of SFF-8431 Rev. 3.04. The optical transceiver is compliant per the RoHS Directive 2011/65/EU[5] . See Finisar Application Note AN-2038 for more details[6] . ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Up to 14.025 Gb/s bi-directionaldata links - Hot-pluggable SFP+ footprint - Tri Rate 4.25 / 8.5 / 14.025 Gb/s Fibre Channel through Rate Select - Built-in digital diagnostic functions - 1310nm DFB laser transmitter - Duplex LC connector - RoHS compliant and Lead Free - Up to 25 km on 9/125µm SMF - Metal enclosure, for lower EMI - 1W maximum power consumption with established link - Single 3.3V power supply - Extended operating temperature range: 0°C to 70°C **FTLF1429P3BCE** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLF1429P3BCE** ## **I.
