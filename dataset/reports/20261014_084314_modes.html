Reach 120 km dispersion limited, optionally extendable to 300 km. Case short-term operating temperature -5°C to 75°C for <96 h. Tx output power stated as >0 dBm in Features; table gives Min 0 dBm. CD tolerance table units are ns/nm (Default max 2.4 at OSNR penalty <0.5 dB; Extended max 6.0 at OSNR penalty <1.0 dB). Rx signal input power (amplified) -18 to 1 dBm; extended range -22 to 3 dBm with <1 dB OSNR penalty relative to Rx OSNR tolerance. Rx OSNR tolerance back-to-back PRx,sig > -18 dBm: 16.5 dB/12.5GHz (100G DQPSK SC), 21.5 dB/12.5GHz (100G DQPSK RS). CFO: up to 1 dB penalty for worst case |CFO| = 3.6 GHz. Power dissipation increases by approximately 0.2 W if extended CD compensation is enabled. High-TX variants QSFP-ZRHF include TOF. OSNR and SMSR referenced per 12.5 GHz bandwidth where noted.
