DATASHEET 5.3 ## **SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCUxM** QSFP28 to SFP28, 100GBase, Direct Attach Cable (DAC), AWG30, 1m to 5m, passive ## OVERVIEW The SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCUxM is a passive 100G to 4x 25G Ethernet Direct Attach Cable (DAC) coax solution. A typical application for a SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCUxM break-out cables is between a QSFP28 port on a 100Gbps switch and feed up to four upstream 25GbE-SFP28 enabled switches. Each QSFP28-SFP28 splitter cable features a single QSFP28 connector (SFF-8665) rated for 100Gbps on one end and 4x SFP28 connectors (SFF-8402), each rated for 25Gbps, on the other. The SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCUxM is provided in lengths from 1 to 5 meters (3.3 to 16.4 ft). Break-out from is directly from QSFP28 side for 2 meters and below. For lengths of 3 meters and above, the break-out from the QSFP28 is 1m. The thickness of the cable is defined by the AWG (American Wire Gauge) rating value. The SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCUxM uses AWG30 and AWG26 rated cables. ## PRODUCT FEATURES - QSFP28 (100G) to 4x SFP28 (25G) Splitter Cable - 1x QSFP28 100 Gbps rated connector (SFF-8665 compliant) - 4x SFP28 25 Gbps rated connectors (SFF-8402 compliant) - SFF-8431 (electrical interfaces) / 8432 (mechanical specifications) compliant - Protocol agnostic support of 1000GbE, EDR InfiniBand - IEEE 802.3bj and P802.3by compliant - 1 to 5 meters (3.3 to 16.4 ft) - AWG 30 and AWG26 (cable thickness) - SFF-8402 SFP28 and SFF-8665 QSFP28 compliant - Fully RoHS compliant for environmental protection - Operating case temperature 0 to +70[o] C ## APPLICATIONS - Data Center cabling infrastructure - Storage Area Network (SAN) - Hub, switches, routers, servers ## INFORMATION **Part number** SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCU1M SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCU2M SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCU3M SO-QSFP28-4SFP28-PCU5M **Description** QSFP28 to 4xSFP28 Direct Attach Cable (DAC), 100G Ethernet, AWG30, 1m, passive QSFP28 to 4xSFP28 Direct Attach Cable (DAC), 100G Ethernet, AWG30, 2m, pas ...
