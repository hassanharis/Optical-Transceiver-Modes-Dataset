DATASHEET 5.0 ## **SO-SFP-155M-L160D-Cxx** SFP, 125Mbps – 155Mbps, CWDM, DDM, 37dB, 160 km, 1270nm – 1610nm (18ch) ## OVERVIEW The TSF037-CxxC-SO is a CWDM transceiver for Fast Ethernet (100M) and STM-1/OC-3 services. The optical performance provides a bridgeable distance of up to 160km depending on the actual optical path attenuation. Note that the lower wavelengths have a higher attenuation on standard SM fiber. The transceiver is available in 18 CWDM wavelength versions, spanning from 1270nm to 1610nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard.
