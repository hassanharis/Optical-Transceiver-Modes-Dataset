Optical Transceivers ## FTLX3671DTCXX ## **Industrial Temperature DWDM 1.2G-11.3G, 40km SFP+ Transceiver** FTLX3671DTCxx transceivers are Enhanced Small Form Factor Pluggable SFP+ transceivers designed for use in DWDM links up to 40km of G.652 single mode fiber, for multi-rate applications from 1.2 through 11.3 Gb/s, carrying 1G-10G Ethernet, 2x-10x Fibre Channel, 10G OTN traffic and CPRI options 2 through 8 traffic. The FTLX3671DTCxx transceivers are compliant with SFF-8431[1] and SFF-8432[2] . Digital diagnostics functions are available via a 2-wire serial interface, as specified in SFF84723. FTLX3671DTCxx transceivers are RoHS compliant per Directive 2011/65/EU 4,5. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - 100 GHz spacing, DWDM C-band - Up to 40km link length - PIN Receiver with Limiting Interface - 1.2 through 11.3 Gb/s data rates - -40/85°C case temperature range - DWDM 40km links for: - 2x through 10x Fibre Channel - 1G through 10G Ethernet - CPRI options 2 through 8 - OTN OTU 1, 2 and 2f - 1.6W max power consumption* - at 85°C - Single 3.3V power supply - Cooled 1550nm EML laser - Hot-pluggable SFP+ footprint - Duplex LC connector - Built-in digital diagnostic functions - RoHS-6 compliant **FTLX3671DTCXX** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLX3671DTCxx** xx=ITU-T Grid Channel (see next page) ## **Product Ordering Codes / Channel Selection** |Channel|Product Code|Frequency<br>(THz)|Center Wave-<br>length<br>(nm)||Channel|Product Code|Frequency<br>(THz)|Center Wave-<br>length<br>(nm)| |---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---| |C20|FTLX3671DTC20|192.00|1561.42||C41|FTLX3671DTC41|194.10|1544.53| |C21|FTLX3671DTC21|192.10|1560.61||C42|FTLX3671DTC42|194.20|1543.73| |C22|FTLX3671DTC22|192.20|1559.79||C43|FTLX3671DTC43|194.30|1542.94| |C23|FTLX3671DTC23|192.30|1558.98||C44|FTLX3671DTC44|194.40|1542.14| |C24|FTLX3671DTC24|192.40|1558.17||C45|FTLX3671DTC45|194.50|1541.35| |C25|FTLX3671DTC25|192.50|1557.36||C46|FT ...
