Notes
Recommended optical/electrical tables (Table 4–6) specified at TC = 0°C to 70°C, VccT/VccR = 3.3V ± 5%; Table 2 case operating temperature max is 85°C. Receiver sensitivity and stressed sensitivity are specified as Input Optical Modulation Amplitude (OMA) in µW (e.g. 95, 89, 76 µW unstressed; 263, 170, 151 µW stressed) rather than dBm. Tx average optical output power table lists minimum Pout only (-6.2, -7.8, -8.2 dBm); max Pout is defined as lesser of Class 1 safety limits or received power max per note. Link distances from EEPROM footnotes: OM4 100m at 32GFC (125m at 16GFC, 190m at 8GFC); OM3 70m at 32GFC (100m at 16GFC, 150m at 8GFC); OM2 20m at 32GFC (35m at 16GFC, 50m at 8GFC); OM1 25m at 8.5 Gb/s. Vertical Eye Closure Penalty (VECP) max 3.13 dB @ 28.05 Gb/s, 2.56 dB @ 14.025 Gb/s; TWDP max 4.3 dB @ 8.5 Gb/s. Stressed receiver ISI (vertical eye closure penalty) min 3.1 dB @ 28.05 Gb/s, 2.5 dB @ 14.025 Gb/s, 3.1 dB @ 8.5 Gb/s. 32GFC requires FEC RS(528,514); 16GFC and 8GFC not compatible with FEC per FC-PI-5.
