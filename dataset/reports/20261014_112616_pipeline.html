DATASHEET 5.0 ## **SO-QSFP-40G-AOCxM** ## QSFP+, 40GBase, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 1m to 100m ## OVERVIEW The SO-QSFP-40G-AOCxM is an Active Optical Cable (AOC) solution for short-range multi-lane data communication and interconnect applications. The solution consists of QSP+ transceivers connected via a ribbon optical cable of desired length for 40Gbps Ethernet, InfiniBand QDR (4x 10Gbps), DDR (4x 5Gbps) and SDR (4x 2.5Gbps) interconnects. SO-QSFP-40G-AOCxM integrates four data lanes (up to 10 Gbps) in each direction with 40 Gbps aggregate bandwidth. The SO-QSFP-40G-AOCxM is provided in lengths from 1 to 100 meters (3.3 to 328 ft). ## PRODUCT FEATURES - 2x QSFP+ transceivers with Multimode ribbon fiber cable - SFF-8436 compliant QSFP connector - 40GBASE-SR4 IEEE 802.3ba compliant - 1 to 100 meters (3.3 to 328 ft) - Fully RoHS compliant for environmental protection - Operating temperature 0[o] C to +70[o] C (32°F to 158°F) - Storage temperature 0[o] C to +85[o] C (32°F to 185°F) ## APPLICATIONS - Data Center cabling infrastructure - Storage Area Network (SAN) - Network Attached Storage (NAS) - Hub, switches, routers, servers ## ORDERING INFORMATION |**Ordering code**|**Description**| |---|---| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC1M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 1m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC2M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 2m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC3M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 3m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC4M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 4m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC5M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 5m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC6M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 6m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC7M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 7m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC8M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 8m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC9M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 9m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC10M|QSFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 40G Ethernet, 10m| |SO-QSFP-40G-AOC15M| ...
