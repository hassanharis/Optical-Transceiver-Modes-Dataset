The dual far reach 8-channel (2x FR4) design uses 200G-PAM4 electrical and optical modulation based on the CWDM4 serial, multiplexed 1310nm wavelength grid. It is the ideal solution for supercomputing and HPC industries, seamlessly integrating into computing and storage infrastructure to ensure efficient high-performance interconnectivity.
