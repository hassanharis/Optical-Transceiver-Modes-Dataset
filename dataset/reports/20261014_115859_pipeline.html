DATASHEET 5.2 ## **SO-QSFP-LX4** ## QSFP+, 40G Ethernet IR4, 1271/1291/1311/1331nm, 2km@SM, 150m@OM3, 4.5dB, LC ## OVERVIEW The SO-QSFP-LX4 is a QSFP+ (Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus) transceiver for 40 Gbps applications such as inter- and intra-connect within and between data centers between switches, routers, storage equipment etc. The SO-QSFP-LX4 converts 4x 10 Gbps flows into four CWDM channels in the 1300nm band up to 2 km over a SingleMode (SM) fiber, up to 150m over an OM3 grade MultiMode (MM) fiber and up to 300m if the more recent OM4 fiber is used.
