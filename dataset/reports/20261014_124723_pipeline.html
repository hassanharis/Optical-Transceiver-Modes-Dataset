DATASHEET 5.0 ## **SO-SFP-1000BASE-BX80D-4955 & -5549** SFP BiDi, 100Mbps - 1.25Gbps, 1490/1550nm, SM, DDM, 26dB, 80km, C-temp & E-temp ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-1000BASE-BX80D is a bi-directional transceiver solution operating directly on a single-fiber without the need for a separate optical filter. This is achieved by having two transceivers that inject different wavelengths into the same single-fiber. The solution thus consists of two transceivers; SO-SFP-1000BASE-BX80D-4955 and SO-SFP-1000BASE-BX80D-5549, operating at 1490nm and 1550nm respectively. Using a single-fiber solution provides a cost-efficient solution for interconnect and it simplifies the patching since no separate transmit/receive direction must be taken into account. The transceiver pair supports the bit rate range from 100Mbps to 1.25Gbps, i.e. Fast Ethernet (FE), Gigabit Ethernet (GbE) and 1Gbps Fiberchannel (1G FC) services. The optical performance of the transceiver pair provides a bridgeable distance of up to 80km. The transceiver solution is available in two temperature range options, one being an extended temperature range of -20°C to +85°C (-4°F to +185°F). The transceivers provide digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification. ## TECHNICAL DATA |**Parameter**|**Value**||**Parameter**|**Value**| |---|---|---|---|---| |Technology|BiDi SFP||**Transmitter data:**|| |Transmission media|SM (1x LC)||Output power|Min: 0.0dBm3)| |Typical reach|80km|||Max: +5.0dBm3)| |Nominal wavelength Tx/Rx|1490nm/1550nm1)& 1550nm/1490nm2)||Transmit wavelength|1484 – 1491nm1)| |Bit rate support|100Mbps to 1.25Gbps|||1544 – 1557nm2)| |Protocol support|FE, GbE||**Receiver data:**|| ||1G FC||Minimum input power|-26.0dBm3) 4)| |Power budget|8 – 26dB||Overload (max power)|-3.0dBm3) 4)| |Dispersion penalty|Max 1dB||Wavelength range|1530 – 1580nm1)| |Power consumption|< 1.0W|||1470 – 1510nm2)| |Operating temperature|0°C to +70°C||LOS Assert|Min -42dBm ...
