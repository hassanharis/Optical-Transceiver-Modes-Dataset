Optical Transceivers ## FTLC1154RDPLC ## **100G 20km eLR4 QSFP28 Optical Transceiver Module (WDM-20)** FTLC1154RDPLC QSFP28 transceiver modules are designed for use in 100 Gigabit Ethernet links on up to 20km of single mode fiber, based on the LAN-WDM wavelength grid. Designed for outside plant applications with an extended temperature range (-20°C to +85°C), they are compliant with the QSFP28 MSA[1] , IEEE 802.3ba 100GBASE-LR4[2] , 4WDM MSA[7] , portions of CWDM4 MSA[8] , and IEEE 802.3bm CAUI-4[6] . Digital diagnostic functions are available via the I2C interface, as specified by the QSFP28 MSA and Finisar Application Notes AN-21524and AN-21535. The optical transceiver is compliant per the RoHS-6 Directive 2011/65/EU[6] . See Finisar Application Note AN-2038[7] for more details. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Hot-pluggable QSFP28 form factor - Supports 103.1Gb/s aggregate bit rate - Power dissipation < 4.5W - 100G 4WDM applications with FEC - Outside Plant - Reduced Air Flow Central Office - RoHS-6 compliant - Extended temperature range of -20°C to 85°C - Single 3.3V power supply - Maximum link length of 20km on Single Mode Fiber (SMF) - 4x25Gb/s DFB-based LAN-WDM transmitter - 4x25G retimed electrical interface - Duplex LC receptacles - I2C management interface **FTLC1154RDPLC** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLC1154RDPLC** - R: 100GE-LR4 maximum bit rate (103.1 Gb/s) D: 4x25G LAN-WDM optical architecture - P: Pull-tab release - L: LC straight receptacles C: Extended reach (20 km), extended temperature ## **I.
