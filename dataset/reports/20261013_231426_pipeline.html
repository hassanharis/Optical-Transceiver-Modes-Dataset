Optical Transceivers ## FTLX2471DC0XX ## **RoHS-6 Compliant CWDM 10Gb/s 10km Single Mode Datacom SFP+ Transceiver** FTLX2471DC0xx 10Gb/s Enhanced Small Form Factor Pluggable SFP+ transceivers are designed for use in CWDM 10km Single Mode fiber links. They are compliant with SFF-8431[1] , SFF-8432[2] and compatible with IEEE 802.3ae 10GBASE_x005F_ xFFFE_LR/LW[3] , and 10G Fibre Channel 1200-SM-LL-L4.
