DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-SFP-10GE-ZR & -ZR-I** SFP+, 10G Multirate, 1550nm, SM, DDM, 23dB, 80km ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-10GE-ZR is a versatile 1550nm transceiver for SingleMode fiber supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The optical performance is in accordance with the industry ZR/ZW-standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 80km for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-ZR) and 10GbE-WAN (10GBASE-ZW) services. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
