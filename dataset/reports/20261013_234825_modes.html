Notes
Copper (1000BASE-T) SFP transceiver over Category 5 UTP, not an optical module. LP-SFP-MSRC is 1000 Mbps only with SERDES host interface, 0~70°C commercial case (ordering row). Features cite 1.05 W typical dissipation; Table 1 notes 1.2 W max power over full voltage and temperature range and states power consumption and surge current are higher than SFP MSA limits. Electrical power section states LP-SFP-xxxx input voltage +5 V +/- 5% and that 3.3 V max is not allowed for continuous operation, while Table 1 is titled +3.3 V electrical power interface (Vcc 3.13–3.47 V). Symbol rate 250 Mbps on each of four cable pairs with 5-level PAM per IEEE 802.3. Line frequency 125 MHz (Table 3). Reach 100 m Category 5 UTP at BER < 10^-12 (Table 5); ordering variants also state Transmission Distance 0.1 Km. Industrial temperature -40 to +85°C applies to other orderable SKUs (e.g. MSRI), not LP-SFP-MSRC.
