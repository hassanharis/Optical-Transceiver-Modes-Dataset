DATASHEET 5.2 ## **SO-SFP-10GE-LR70-Cxx** SFP+, 10G Multirate, CWDM, DDM, 23dB, 1270nm-1450nm (10ch) ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-10GE-LR70-Cxx is a versatile CWDM transceiver supporting a wide range of traffic formats ranging from 0.614Gbps to 11.3Gbps. The optical performance provides a power budget of 23dB giving a distance of up to 70km depending on used wavelength. Please note the higher intrinsic fiber attenuation in the 1271-1451nm area. The transceiver is available in 10 CWDM wavelength versions, spanning from 1270nm to 1450nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard.
