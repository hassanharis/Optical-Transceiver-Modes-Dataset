The optical performance provides a power budget of 14dB giving a distance of up to 40km depending on used wavelength. Please note the higher intrinsic fiber attenuation in the 1271-1451nm area. The transceiver is available in 10 CWDM wavelength versions, spanning from 1270nm to 1450nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard.
