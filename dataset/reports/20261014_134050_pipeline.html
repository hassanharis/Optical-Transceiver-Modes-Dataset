DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-SFP-10GE-LR & -LR-I** SFP+, 10G Multirate, 1310nm, SM, DDM, 6.2dB, 10km ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-10GE-LR is a versatile 1310nm transceiver supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The optical performance is in accordance with the IEEE 802.3ae LR/LW-standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 10km for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-LR) and 10GbE-WAN (10GBASE-LW) services. The transceiver has no minimum distance (i.e. no minimum attenuation) which is ideal for intra-office connections since extra attenuators need not be considered. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification. The transceiver is available in two temperature range options, one being the Industrial temperature range (I-temp): -40°C to 85°C (-40°F to 185°F). ## TECHNICAL DATA |**Parameter**|**Value**||**Parameter**|**Value**| |---|---|---|---|---| |Technology|Grey SFP+||**Transmitter data:**|| |Transmission media|SM (2x LC)||Output power|Min: -8.2dBm1)| |Typical reach|10km|||Max: +0.5dBm1)| |Nominal wavelength|1310nm||Transmit wavelength|1270nm – 1355nm| |Bit rate support|0.6Gbps to 11.3Gbps||**Receiver data:**|| |Interface standards|10GBASE-LR, 10GBASE-LW||Minimum input power|-14.4dBm1) 2)| |Protocol support|GbE, 10GbE-LAN, 10GbE-WAN||Overload (max power)|+0.5dBm1)| ||OTU1, OTU2, OTU2e||Wavelength range|1260nm – 1565nm| ||STM-64/OC192||LOS assert|Min -28dBm| ||STM-16/OC48, STM-4/OC12||LOS de-assert|Max -16dBm| ||1G, 2G, 4G, 8G, 10G FC||DDM|Yes| ||CPRI Opt, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7, 7A, 8||MSA compliance|SFF-8431, -8432, -8472| ||OBSAI 1x, 2x, 4x, 8x|||| |Power budget|0 – 6.2dB|||| |Dispersion penalty|Max 3.2dB||1)Average power.|| |Power consumption|< 1W||2)@ 10.3Gbps, BER 1x10-12, PRBS 231-1, back-to-back.|| |Operating temperature|-0°C to +70°C (-LR)|||| ||-40°C to +85°C (-LR-I)|||| |Storage temperature|-40°C to +85°C|||| |**Safety/regulatory compliance:**||||| |TUV/UL/FDA (contact Smartoptics for ...
