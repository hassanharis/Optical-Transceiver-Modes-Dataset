Active Optical Cable (AOC): two QSFP28 transceivers with multimode ribbon fiber; not a pluggable optical module datasheet. FEC required in host per IEEE802.3bj (Reed Solomon RS(528,514)). Without FEC, BER less than 5 x 10^-5; with FEC, BER better than 1 x 10^-12. Power stated as <2.5W. Cable lengths 1–70 m (3.3–230 ft). Storage temperature -40°C to +85°C stated separately from operating 0°C to +70°C.
