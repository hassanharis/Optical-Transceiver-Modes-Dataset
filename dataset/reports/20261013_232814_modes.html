Notes
Bi-directional (simplex SC) 1×9 module; order row: 1310nm FP Tx, PIN Rx, 3.3V, LVPECL TX/RX and LVPECL SD. Launch power, sensitivity, and maximum input power rows are annotated 20km. Central wavelength 1270–1350 nm (1310 FP); output spectrum width Δλ max 4 nm (FP-LD). Data rate in recommended operating conditions: Symbol B, 1250 Mbps typ, 1500 Mbps max (Min column shows 10 Mbps). Supply current Icc max 260 mA (recommended) and transmitter Icc max 250 mA. Signal detect range −38 to −23 dBm; SD low when no light. Features also mention up to 0.55 km on 62.5/125 μm MMF and up to 60 km on 9/125 μm SMF (family/features, not tied to 20 km order reach). Optical rise/fall time max 2.0 ns (20%–80%). Transmitter differential input voltage 550–1750 mV; Rin typ 100 Ω.
