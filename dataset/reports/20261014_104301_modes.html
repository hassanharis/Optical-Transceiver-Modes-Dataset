Notes
Product footer lists XGIGA Communication Technology Co.,ltd as contact; module branding is Amphenol ICC Optical Module. Transmission distances are stated in meters (e.g. ordering 100 m OM4/OM5; 64GFC up to 70 m OM3, 100 m OM4/OM5). 64GFC BER range 10^-10 to 1.31x10^-4 with PRBS31Q per note 1; 32GFC/16GFC BER 10^-12 with PRBS31 per note 2. Receiver sensitivities and launch powers are often specified as OMAouter or OMA, not average power only. 64GFC Damage Threshold receiver tolerance 5 dBm average (note 1: tolerance without required correct operation). 16GFC max average launched power is lesser of table value or Class 1 laser safety limits.
