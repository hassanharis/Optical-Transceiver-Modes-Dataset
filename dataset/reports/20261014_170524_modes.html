Notes
Copper (10GBASE-T) module over twisted pair, not optical. Cable reach stated in meters only: 10Gbase-T CAT6A 30m; 5Gbase-T, 2.5Gbase-T, 1000base-T, 100base-Tx CAT5E 100m (rev 2.0 updated 2.5G/5G reach to 100m). General data rate 1–10 Gb/sec IEEE 802.3; line port supports 10G/5G/2.5G/1000BASE-T; host port 10GBase-R. Power consumption and surge current exceed SFP MSA limits (max 3.6 W, supply current typ 900 mA max 1100 mA). Case operating temperature 0°C to +70°C; storage -40°C to +85°C. LOS: high = no link, low = linked; RX_LOS used for link indication on 10GBASE-T. Line electrical: 5-level encoding per IEEE 802.3, fL 125 MHz typ.
