Notes
32GFC (28.05 Gb/s) requires FEC RS(528,514) per FC-PI-6; 16GFC and 8GFC are not compatible with FEC per FC-PI-5. Unstressed receiver sensitivity is specified as minimum input OMA (72 µW at 28.05 Gb/s, 63 µW at 14.025 Gb/s, 42 µW at 8.5 Gb/s), not as rx_sensitivity_dbm. Max average Pout for 14.025 Gb/s and 8.5 Gb/s is limited per Note 1 (Class 1 safety or received power max) without a numeric max in Table 7 for those rows. Transmitter Distortion Penalty (TDP) max: 2.7 dB at 28.05 Gb/s, 4.4 dB at 14.025 Gb/s, 3.2 dB at 8.5 Gb/s—not mapped to penalty schema (no paired penalized OSNR/sensitivity at stated penalty dB and conditions). EEPROM lists 1 W power class; Table 5 transceiver power consumption max 1.2 W. Case operating temperature recommended 0°C to 70°C; absolute max case -40°C to 85°C.
