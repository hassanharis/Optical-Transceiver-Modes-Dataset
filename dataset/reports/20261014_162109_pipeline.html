DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-TXFP-10G-ZR-DWDM-A** XFP, 10G Multirate, DWDM, 50GHz Tunable, 25dB, 80km, D9135-D9610 (96ch) ## OVERVIEW The SO-TXFP-10G-ZR-DWDM-A is a high performance DWDM XFP-transceiver that is tunable to 96 channels in the 50GHz C- band grid as specified in ITU-T 694.1. The mechanical characteristics are compliant with the XFP MSA-specification. Wavelength and frequency tuning modes are supported in accordance with SFF-8477. The transceiver supports data rates from 9.95 to 11.3 Gbps, covering a series of Ethernet, OTN, SDH/SONET and other protocols. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the INF 8077i specification.
