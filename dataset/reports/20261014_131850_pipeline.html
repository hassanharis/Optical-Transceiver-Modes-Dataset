DATASHEET 5.2 ## **SO-SFP-100BASE-T & -T-I** SFP, 10/100Base-T, 100m, RJ45, C-temp & I-temp ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-100BASE-T is a transceiver with a high-performance integrated duplex data link for bidirectional communication over copper cable.
