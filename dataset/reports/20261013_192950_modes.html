Notes
Forward error correction (FEC) is required to be implemented by the host. Case operating temperature 0°C to 70°C without air flow. Receiver sensitivity note 2: measured with PRBS31Q test pattern, BER@2.4*10^-4. Transmit OMA per lane (min) and receiver sensitivity (OMA) per lane are conditional on TDECQ/TECQ (<1.4 dB vs 1.4dB≤TDECQ≤3.4dB): min launch -0.2 dBm for TDECQ<1.4dB; -1.6+TDECQ dBm for 1.4dB≤TDECQ≤3.4dB; RXsens -4.6 dBm for TECQ<1.4 dB; -6+TECQ dBm for 1.4dB≤TECQ≤3.4dB. Wavelength assignment 1304.5–1317.5 nm suitable for all channels (note 3). Link power budget 7.8 dB typ for maximum TDECQ; allocation for penalties 3.8 dB for maximum TDECQ. LPO module does not have a CDR (VI Notes). Power dissipation typical 8 W, <8.5 W. Optical receiver damage threshold input 5.4 dBm (absolute max); damage threshold per lane 5.4 dBm (receiver).
