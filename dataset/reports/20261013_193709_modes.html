Notes
Dual SFP-DD data paths (legacy SFP+ channel and DD channel); rates selected via Rate Select and I2C. 64GFC uses PAM4 at 28.9 GBd with RS(544,514) FEC per FC-PI-7; 32GFC NRZ 28.05 GBd with RS(528,514); 16GFC NRZ 14.025 GBd with no FEC per FC-PI-5. Many optical limits are OMAouter or average power per mode (Tables 10–15). 64GFC receiver sensitivity and stressed sensitivity are OMAouter at BER 1.09E-4. 32GFC/16GFC sensitivities are input OMA (peak-to-peak) with Fibre Channel encoded input. Max transmitter average power for 32GFC/16GFC is lesser of Class 1 laser limits or receive power max. Storage temperature –40°C to 85°C (absolute max) vs case operating 0°C to 75°C. No OSNR or reach (km) specifications stated.
