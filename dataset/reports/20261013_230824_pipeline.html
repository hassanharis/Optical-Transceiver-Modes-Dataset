Optical Transceivers ## FTLF8536W4BTV ## **25.78Gb/s 10GE RoHS Compliant Short Wavelength SFP+ Transceiver** FTLF8536W4BTV SFP+ transceivers are designed for use in 25Gb/s data rate over multimode fiber. They are compliant to IEEE802.3by, SFF-8472 Rev 12.2b and SFF-8402g, and compatible with SFF-8432a and applicable portions of SFF-8431 Rev. 4.1c. The transceiver is RoHS compliant and per Directive 2011/65/EUd . See Finisar Application Note AN-2038 for more details. ## FEATURES ## APPLICATIONS - Up to 25.78 Gb/s bi-directional data links - Hot-pluggable SFP+ footprint - Built-in digital diagnostic functions - 25GE SR/eCPRI - CPRI option 10, 7 , 8 - 100GE SR fan out to 4x25GE SR - 850nm Oxide VCSEL laser transmitter - Duplex LC connector - RoHS compliant - 100m over M5F MMF (50/125 um OM4) - 70m over M5E MMF (50/125um OM3) - 20m on MMF (50/125μm OM2) - Metal enclosure, for lower EMI - 1.2W maximum power consumption - Single 3.3V power supply - Operating temperature range: -40°C to 85°C **FTLF8536W4BTV** ## **Product Selection** ## **FTLF8536W4BTV** ## **I.
