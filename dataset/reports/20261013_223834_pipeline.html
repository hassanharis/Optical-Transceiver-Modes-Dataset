Finisar Transceiver # **Preliminary Product Specification 800G (2x400 DR4) OSFP Optical Finisar Transceiver** ## **FTCE4517E1PxA-2N** ## **PRODUCT FEATURES** - Hot-pluggable Type 2 OSFP form factor - Supports 850Gb/s aggregate bit rate - Power dissipation <17W - RoHS-6 compliant - Case temperature range of 20°C to +60°C (limited temp) or 0°C to +70°C (c-temp) ## **APPLICATIONS** - Single 3.3V power supply - Aligned with IEEE 802.3bs - 8x100G PAM4 retimed 106.25Gb/s PAM4 electrical interface aligned to IEEE 802.3ck - 8 x 100GbE applications - 2 x 400GbE applications - 4 x 200Gbe applications - NDR Infiniband - DUAL MPO-12, APC receptacles - I2C management interface The FTCE4517E1PxA-2N (2 x DR4) OSFP transceiver modules are designed for use in (2 x 400) Gigabit Ethernet links on up to 500m of single mode fiber. They are compliant with the OSFP MSA, IEEE 802.3bs[6 ] and IEEE P802.3ck[7] Digital diagnostic functions are available via the I2C interface, as specified by the OSFP MSA. The optical transceiver is RoHS compliant as described in Application Note AN-2038[2,3] . . ## **PRODUCT SELECTION** E: Ethernet protocol P: Pull-tab type release x: C = Commercial or L limited temperature A: Dual MPO-12 receptacle 2N : No Heat Sink Design © Coherent Corp.
