DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-QSFP-IR4-PSM** ## QSFP+, 40G Ethernet IR4, SM, 1310nm, 2km, 7.4dB, MPO (APC) ## OVERVIEW The SO-QSFP-IR4-PSM is a QSFP+ (Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus) transceiver for 40Gbps applications such as inter- and intra-connect within and between data centers between switches, routers, storage equipment etc. The SO-QSFP-IR4-PSM converts 4x 10Gbps flows into four channels at 1310nm up to 2 km over a SingleMode (SM) fiber. The transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
