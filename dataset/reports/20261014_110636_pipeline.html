DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-CFP-40GBASE-LR4** ## CFP, 40Gbps Ethernet LR4, SDH/SONET, OTN, SM, DDM, 6.7dB, 10km ## OVERVIEW The SO-CFP-40GBASE-LR4 is a CFP (C Form-factor Pluggable) transceiver for 40Gbps applications. It is intended for use in interand intra-connect applications within and between data centers between switches, routers, storage equipment etc. The optical performance is in accordance with the 40GBASE-LR standard, i.e. for optical distances up to 10km over a SingleMode (SM) fiber.
