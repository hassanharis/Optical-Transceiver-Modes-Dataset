Recommended case temperature 0 °C to 70 °C (Table 5). Nominal serial line rate for 16GFC is 14.025 Gb/s; Tx/Rx CDR engaged only when Rate Select is high (16GFC), bypassed for 8.5 Gb/s and 4.25 Gb/s. Transmitter and receiver may operate at different data rates during FC speed negotiation. Average optical output power maximum is the lesser of Class 1 safety limits or received power maximum (Table 8 Note 1). Receiver sensitivity and stressed sensitivity are specified as input OMA in µW (89/76/61 µW unstressed; 170/151/148/138/126 µW stressed per Table 9), not as average power in dBm. Link reach values are stated per fiber type and data rate in EEPROM Table 12 notes (meters). Module supply current maximum 300 mA (Table 6); power level 1 operation 1 W max (Table 17). Supply voltage 3.135 V to 3.465 V recommended (Table 5).
