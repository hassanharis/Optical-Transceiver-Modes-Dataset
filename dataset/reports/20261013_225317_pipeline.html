# **Product Specification 10km 100G eCWDM4 QSFP28 Optical Transceiver Module (4WDM-10)** ## **FTLC1157RGPLA** ## **PRODUCT FEATURES** - Hot-pluggable QSFP28 form factor - Supports 103.1Gb/s aggregate bit rate - Power dissipation < 3.5W - RoHS-6 compliant - Commercial case temperature range of 0°C to +70°C - Single 3.3V power supply - Maximum link length of 10km on Single Mode Fiber (SMF) [with KR4 FEC] - 4x25Gb/s CWDM transmitter ## **APPLICATIONS** - 100G 4WDM applications with FEC - 4x25G retimed electrical interface - Duplex LC receptacles - I2C management interface Finisar’s FTLC1157RGPLA QSFP28 transceiver modules are designed for use in 100 Gigabit Ethernet links on up to 10 km of single mode fiber, based on the CWDM4 wavelength grid. Designed for data center applications, the transceivers are compliant with the QSFP28 MSA[1] , CWDM4 MSA[2] , 4WDM MSA[3] , and portions of IEEE P802.3bm CAUI-4[4] . Digital diagnostic functions are available via the I2C interface, as specified by the QSFP28 MSA and Finisar Application Notes AN-2153[5] and AN-2154[6] . The transceiver is RoHS-6 compliant and lead-free per Directive 2011/65/EC[7] and Finisar Application Note AN-2038[8] . ## **PRODUCT SELECTION** ## **FTLC1157RGPLA** R: Ethernet maximum bit rate (103.1 Gb/s) G: 4x25G CWDM optical architecture P: Pull-tab release L: LC straight receptacles A: Extended reach (10 km) © Finisar Corporation – 07-Jan-2020 Rev.
