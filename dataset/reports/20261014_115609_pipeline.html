DATASHEET 5.1 ## **SO-QSFP-LR4-PSM** ## QSFP+, 40G Ethernet LR4, SM, 1310nm, 10km, 6.6dB, MPO (APC) ## OVERVIEW The SO-QSFP-LR4-PSM is a QSFP+ (Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus) transceiver for 40 Gbps applications where the transport is made using four channels at 10 Gbps. It is intended for use in inter- and intra-connect applications within and between data centers with switches, routers, storage equipment etc. The transceiver can also be used for 10GbE-LAN interconnect applications, providing a higher density as compared to four individual 10G connections using e.g. SFP+ transceivers.
