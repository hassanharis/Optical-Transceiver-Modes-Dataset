DATASHEET 5.3 ## **SO-SFP-1000BASE-TR & -TR-I** SFP, 1G Ethernet, 100m@CAT5, RJ45 ## OVERVIEW The SO-SFP-1000BASE-TR is a transceiver with a high-performance integrated duplex data link for bidirectional communication over copper cable. It is specifically designed for high speed communication links that require 1G Ethernet over LAN cable. SO-SFP-1000BASE-TR is a solution for 1000 Mbps Ethernet (GbE) connections within racks and across adjacent racks where the interconnected equipment uses SFP interfaces instead of RJ45.
