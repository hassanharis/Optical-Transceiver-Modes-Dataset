## **RJS-ST31 Optical Transceiver** 4x / 2x / 1x Fiber Channel Applications, 850nm Micro Module 4.25 /2.125 / 1.0625 GBaud ## **Applications** The Cinch Connectivity Solutions RJS-ST31 transceiver module is a high performance integrated full duplex data link for bi-directional communication over multimode optical fiber. It has been designed for use in space constrained applications, and offers the mandatory FC compliance commonly provided by SFF and SFP transceivers. This optoelectronic transceiver module is a Class 1 Laser product compliant with FDA Radiation Performance Standards, 21 CFR Subchapter J. This component is also Class 1 Laser compliant according to International Safety Standard IEC-825-1/ EN 60825. ## **Features** - 4.25 GBaud Fiber Channel Compliant - 2.125 GBaud Fiber Channel Compliant - 1.0625 GBaud Fiber Channel Compliant - 100 differential DC coupled inputs/outputs - Industry Standard LC Connector Interface - Metal housing - Single +3.3V Power Supply - RoHS Compliant +1 321.308.4100 ccsorders@us.cinch.com **belfuse.com/cinch** Please read our datasheet & drawing disclaimer here. ## **Ordering Information** ## **R S - ST3 1** Communications Protocol S – 4XFC / 2XFC / 1XFC 4.25 / 2.125 / 1.0625 GBaud Wavelength 1=850nm (multimode) ## **Module Specifications – Electrical:** 0ºC<Tc<+70ºC, +3.135<Vcc<+3.465V **----- Start of picture text -----**<br> Parameter Symbol MIN Typical MAX Unit Notes<br>Supply Current 125 195 mA 0ºC<Tc<+70ºC; +3.135V<Vcc<+3.465V<br>Transmitter<br> Input Swing (Differential) Vin 500 2200 mVpp<br> Input Impedance (Differential) [1] Rin 100 Ω Internally terminated<br> TX_DISABLE Input Voltage – High VIH 2 3.465 V<br> TX_DISABLE Input Voltage – Low VIL 0 0.8 V<br>Receiver<br> Output Swing (Differential) 300 1200 mVpp<br> Output Impedance (Differential) Rout 100 Ω<br> Single Detect Output Voltage – High [2] VroH Vcc-0.5 Vcc+0.3 V Io = 400µA; Host Vcc<br> Single Detec ...
