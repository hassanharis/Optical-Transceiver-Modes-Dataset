2 · Power–performance trade-off

Power and required OSNR for the same 15 modes.

Result: power steps from 18.0 W (100G) to 23.4 W (300–400G) while required OSNR varies with rate, FEC and Enhanced mode (22.5 to 26.0 dB at 400G). Why it needs this dataset: each mode carries its own power and OSNR, so the energy and link-budget cost of a configuration can be read directly.
corpus-level: all datasheets
