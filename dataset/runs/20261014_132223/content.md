## **SO-SFP-10GE-AOCxM** 

SFP+, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 1m to 100m 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-10GE-AOCxM is an Active Optical Cable (AOC) solution. SO-SFP-10GE-AOCxM is a direct-attach assembly (i.e. SFP+’ connectors with integrated cable) for quick and simple 10 Gbps connections within racks and across adjacent racks where the interconnected equipment uses SFP+ interfaces. It provides a cost-efficient solution as compared to using discrete optical transceivers and optical patch cables. 

A SO-SFP-10GE-AOCxM fits into SFP+ ports and support a wide range of Ethernet and Fiber Channel services. The SO-SFP-10GE-AOCxM utilizes a MultiMode fiber and is provided in lengths from 1 to 100 meters. 

## PRODUCT FEATURES 

- SFP+ MSA compliant (SFF-8431 & SFF-8432) 

- Up to 100m interconnects 

- RoSH 6 compliant 

- Multi-service support; 4G, 8G Fiber Channel, 10G Gigabit Ethernet, InfiniBand QDR, SDR, DDR 

- Operating case temperature 0°C to +70°C (+32°F to +158°F) 

- Hot pluggable 

- Storage temperature -40°C to +85°C 

- Bend radius minimum 30mm 

## APPLICATIONS 

- Data Center cabling infrastructure 

- Storage Area Network (SAN) 

- Network Attached Storage (NAS) 

- Hub, switches, routers, servers, Network Interface Cards (NICs)
