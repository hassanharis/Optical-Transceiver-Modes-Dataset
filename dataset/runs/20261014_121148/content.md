## **SO-QSFP28-AOCxM** 

## QSFP28, 100GBase, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 1m to 70m 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-QSFP28-AOCxM is an Active Optical Cable (AOC) solution for short-range multi-lane data communication and interconnect applications. The solution consists of QSP28 transceivers connected via a ribbon optical cable of desired length for 100Gbps Ethernet and InfiniBand interconnects. SO-QSFP28-AOCxM integrates four data lanes (25Gbps) in each direction with 100 Gbps aggregate bandwidth. 

Forward Error Correction (FEC) is required in the host equipment to ensure reliable system operation. The FEC type shall be as defined in IEEE802.3bj, i.e. Reed Solomon RS(528,514). Without FEC the bit error ratio (BER) will be less than 5 x10[-5] . With FEC the BER will be better than 1 x 10[-12] . 

The SO-QSFP28-AOCxM is provided in lengths from 1 to 70 meters (3.3 to 230 ft). 

## PRODUCT FEATURES 

- 2x QSFP28 transceivers with Multimode ribbon fiber cable 

- 100GbE 

- 100G InfiniBand (EDR) 

- SFF-8665 compliant QSFP28 connector 

- Digital diagnostics functions via the I2C interface 

- 1 to 70 meters (3.3 to 230 ft) 

- Fully RoHS compliant for environmental protection 

- Low power consumption; <2.5W 

- Operating temperature 0[o] C to +70[o] C (32°F to +158°F) 

- Storage temperature -40[o] C to +85[o] C (-40°F to +185°F) 

## APPLICATIONS

- Storage Area Network (SAN) 

- Network Attached Storage (NAS)
