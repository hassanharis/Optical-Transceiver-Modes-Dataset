## **SO-SFP-L120D-Dxxx & Dxxx-E** 

SFP, 0.1 - 1.25Gbps, DWDM, 100GHz, DDM, 32dB, 120km, D9190-D9600 (42ch) 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-L120D-Dxxx is a DWDM transceiver for services from 100 Mbps (Fast Ethernet) up to 1.25 Gbps (Gigabit Ethernet). 

The optical performance provides a bridgeable distance of up to 120km. The transceiver is provided in 42 channel versions at the 100GHz DWDM grid as specified in the ITU-T 694.1 standard. 

This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification. The transceiver is available in two temperature range options, one being an extended temperature range (E-temp): -20°C to +75°C (-4°F to +167°F). 

The transceiver module is compliant to RoHS-6/6
