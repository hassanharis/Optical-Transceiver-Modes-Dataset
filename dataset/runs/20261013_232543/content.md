## **L9-SC315501-60CTC** 

## 1×9 155Mbps Bi-Directional Transceiver Tx1310/Rx1550 SC 60km Reach 

## **Features** ： 

- Integrated single fiber bi-directional optical subassembly 

- Operating data rate up to 155Mbps 

- Simplex SC optical interface 

- Single +3.3V Power Supply 

- Multi-sourced 1×9 package 

- Very low EMI and certified ESD protection 

- Compliant with ROHS 

- CML Differential Input/Output and (LV)TTL signal detect 

- Operating case temperature range (Standard：0 to +70°C) or (Industrial： -40°C to +85°C) 

## **Applications:** 

- WDM Application 

- SDH STM-1/ SONET OC-3 

- Fast Ethernet 

## **Standard** ： 

- Compliant with ITU-T G.957
