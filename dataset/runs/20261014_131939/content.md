## **SO-SFP-100BASE-ZXD** 

SFP, 100 - 155Mbps, 100Base-ZXD, 1550nm, SM, DDM, 29dB, 80km 

## OVERVIEW 

SO-SFP-100BASE-ZXD is a 1550nm SFP high performance transceiver for SingleMode (SM) fiber for 100Mbps Ethernet (FE) and STM-1/OC-3 services.  The optical performance provides a bridgeable distance of up to 80km. 

This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
