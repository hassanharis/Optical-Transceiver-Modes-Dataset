## **SO-SFP-10G-ZR100** 

SFP+ 10G Multirate 0.6-11.3Gbps 1550nm 100km 26dB 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-10G-ZR100 is an SFP+ form-factor transceiver for multiple applications. The transceiver supports a bit rate range from 600Mbps to 11.3Gbps enabling a wide span of protocols to be carried. The optical performance supports distances up to 100km over a SingleMode (SM) fiber without dispersion compensation. 

SO-SFP-10G-ZR100 provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification. The transceiver supports the commercial temperature range (C-temp): 0°C to +70°C (32°F to +158°F).
