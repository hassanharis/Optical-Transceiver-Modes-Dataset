# **100GBASE-ZR4 QSFP28 1310nm 80km DDM SMF Transceiver** 

# **P/N: QT-QSFP28-ZR4** 

## **Product Features** 

- Hot pluggable QSFP28 MSA form factor 

- Compliant to Ethernet 100GBASE-ZR4 Lite 

- Supports 103.1Gb/s aggregate bit rate 

- Up to 80km reach for G.652 SMF with FEC 

- Single +3.3V power supply 

- Operating case temperature: 0~70oC 

- Transmitter: cooled 4x25Gb/s LAN WDM EML TOSA (1295.56, 1300.05,1304.58,1309.14nm) 

- Receiver: 4x25Gb/s APD ROSA 

- 4x25G electrical interface (OIF CEI-28G-VSR) 

- Maximum power consumption 4.5W 

- Duplex LC receptacle 

- RoHS-6 compliant 

## **Applications** 

- 100GBASE-ZR4 Ethernet Links 

- Infiniband QDR and DDR interconnects 

- Client-side 100G Telecom connections
