## **SO-SFP-16GFC-LD** 

## SFP+, 16/8/4 Gbps FC/FICON, 1310nm, SM, DDM, 6.9dB, 10km 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-16GFC-LD is a versatile tri-rate 1310nm transceiver for SingleMode (SM) fiber and 4G, 8G and 16G Fiber channel services. The optical performance provides a bridgeable distance of up to 10 km in accordance with the 1600-SM-LC-L interface specification within the FC-PI-5 standard. 

The transceiver has no minimum distance (i.e. no minimum attenuation) which is ideal for intra-office connections since extra attenuators need not be considered. 

This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
