## **LS-DAC1110-xxN** 

## RoHS Compliant 10Gb/s SFP+ Copper Cable 

## **Features** 

- Compliant with SFF-8431, 8432 and 8472. 

- Up to 10.3125Gbps data rate per channel 

- Up to 7m transmission 

- Operating temperature:-40℃to +85℃ 

- Single 3.3V power supply 

- RoHS compliant 

## **Benefits** 


- Cost-effective copper solution 

- Lowest total system power solution 

- Lowest total system EMI solution 

- Optimized design for Signal Integrity 

## **Applications** 

- 10G Ethernet 

## **General Description** 

SFP+ Direct Attach Cables are compliant with the SFF-8431, SFF-8432 and SFF-8472 specifications. Various choices of wire gauge are available from 30 to 24 AWG with various choices of cable length (up to 7m).
