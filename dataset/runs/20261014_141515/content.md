## **SO-SFP-155M-L80D-Cxx** 

SFP, 100 - 155Mbps, CWDM, DDM, 29dB, 80 km, 1270nm-1610nm (18ch) 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-155M-L80D-Cxx is a CWDM transceiver for 155 Mbps SDH/SONET and Fast Ethernet services (100M). The optical performance provides a bridgeable distance of up to 80 km. 

The transceiver is available in 18 CWDM wavelengths, spanning from 1270nm to 1610nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
