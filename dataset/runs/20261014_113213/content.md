## **SO-QSFP-4X10G-AOCxM** 

QSFP+ to 4xSFP, 40GBase, Active Optical Cable (AOC), 1m to 100m 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-QSFP-4X10G-AOCxM is an Active Optical Cable (AOC) solution for short-range multi-lane data communication and interconnect applications. The solution consists of 1x QSP+ and 4x SFP+ transceivers with an optical break-out cable of desired length, providing the ability to aggregate 4 discrete SFP+ 10Gbps channels into a single parallel QSFP+ 40Gbps interface. 

The SO-QSFP-4X10G-AOCxM is provided in lengths from 1 to 100 meters (3.3 to 328 ft). 

## PRODUCT FEATURES 

- QSFP+ (40G) to 4x SFP+ (10G) splitter cable 

- 1x QSFP 40.0 Gbps rated connector (SFF-8436 compliant) 

- 4x SFP+ 10.0 Gbps rated connectors (SFF-8431/8432 compliant) 

- 1 to 100 meters (3.3 to 328 ft) 

- Fully RoHS compliant for environmental protection 

- Operating temperature 0[o] C to +70[o] C (32°F to 158°F) 

- Storage temperature 0[o] C to +85[o] C (32°F to 185°F) 

## APPLICATIONS 

- Data Center cabling infrastructure 

- Storage Area Network (SAN) 

- Network Attached Storage (NAS) 

- Hub, switches, routers, servers
