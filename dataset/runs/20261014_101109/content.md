## **OSP1-3120NxR** 

Low Rate 1Mb/s SFP Optical Transceiver Module, Single Mode, 1310nm, 20km Reach 

## **Features** 

- ●Compliant with SFP MSA standard 

- ●Up to 1Mb/s data rate 

- ●Module integrates receive and transmit circuits of two parts 

- ●All-metal shell and the shell with plastic end plug form, LC receptacle. 

- ●Hot-pluggable 

- ●Single +3.3V power supply 

- ●Standard PECL data output and input with signal detect indication 

- ●High quality 1310nm VCSEL 

- ●ROHS compliant and Lead Free 

- ●Operating Temperature: Standard 0~70ºC Industrial -40~85ºC 

## **Applications** 

- ●Industrial Ethernet Applications: industrial Ethernet Switches,Media Converters,Industrial fiber converters 

- ●Serial Communications over fiber: Industrial RS-232/RS-422/RS-485 serial to fiber media converter,industrial RS-232 to RS-422 and RS-485 Adapters 

- ●RS232/RS485/RS422 optical-electrical converter for electric power control, industrial control,industrial computing 

## **Description** 

The OSP1-3120NxR family of SFP optical transceivers from Optcore provide the system designer and manufacturers with products to implement a range of industrial control, electric power control, special low data rate Ethernet designs at the 0-1Mb/s rate. To avoid the disadvantages of the traditional TTL 1x9 transceiver, this transceiver fully complies with the SFP Multi-Sourcing Agreement (MSA) and supports hot-pluggable. The 0~1M SFP transceiver is usually used for serial communications over fiber like RS-232/485 optical-electrical converter and industrial Ethernet networks to fiber networks communications like industrial fiber optic converters and industrial Ethernet switches. The 0~1M SFP optics are provided with commercial temperature grade and industrial temperature grade for different applications. 

There are two versions of the series SFP optical transceiver modules for different applications. The Standard grade (0~70ºC) is for commonly commercial application, and the Industrial grade (-40~85ºC) is made with robust and reliable components to meet the needs of Industrial Ethernet application under harsh environmental conditions.

## **Warnings** 

## _**Process plug**_ 

The transceiver optics is supplied with a dust cover. This plug protects the transceiver optics during standard manufacturing processes by preventing contamination from air borne particles.It is recommended that the dust cover remain in the transceiver whenever an optical fiber connector is not inserted. 

## _**Handling Precautions**_ 

The transceiver optics is susceptible to damage as a result of electrostatic discharge (ESD). A static free environment is highly recommended. Follow guidelines according to proper ESD procedures. 

## _**Laser Safety**_ 

The transceiver optics is a Class 1 laser product per international standard IEC 60825-1. Radiation emitted by laser devices can be dangerous to human eyes. Avoid eye exposure to direct or indirect radiation.
