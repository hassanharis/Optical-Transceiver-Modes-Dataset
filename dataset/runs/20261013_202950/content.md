## **CVR-CFP-QSFP28** 

CFP to QSFP28 converter module 

## OVERVIEW 

The CVR-CFP-QSFP28 provides the ability to use a QSFP28 transceiver in a host having an CFP interface. The CVR-CFP-QSFP28 converts between the bidirectional 10x 10G flows in the CFP and the bidirectional 4x 25G flows in the QSFP28 transceiver. 

The converter does not include a RS (Reed-Solomon) FEC encode and decode functionality, so the converter is to be used with QSFP28 transceivers with interfaces that do not require FEC, such as 100GbE ER4/LR4.and OTU4.
