## **SO-SFP-155M-L120D-C51** 

## SFP, 100/155Mbps, CWDM, DDM, 34dB, 1510nm 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-155M-L120D-C51 is a 1510nm CWDM transceiver for OSC channels in the Smartoptics DCP systems. The optical performance provides a power budget of 34dB. 

The transceiver is available in the 1510nm CWDM wavelength, in accordance with the G.694.2 standard. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the SFF-8472 specification.
