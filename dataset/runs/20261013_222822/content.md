## **FXC-SFP-ZX-1G-CAN** 


## **Features** 

- Operating Data Rate up to 1.25 Gbps 

- Distance Range 80KM 

- Single 3.3V Power Supply and TTL Logic Interface 

- Pluggable SFP Duplex LC Connector 

- Standard and Industrial Operating Temperature 

- Compliant with Ciena SFP Specification 

## **Applications** 

- Telecom (Service Provider) 

- Datacom 

   - Government 

   - Fiber to the home/business 

- Enterprise Networks 

## **Description** 

The L-com FXC-SFP-ZX-1G-CAN is an SFP form-factor transceiver, supporting 1G Ethernet rates. The L-com FXC-SFP-ZX-1G-CAN supports 80KM distance and it is Ciena compliant transceiver. The L-com FXC-SFP-ZX-1G-CAN features digital diagnostics for performance monitoring of the transceiver. The L-com FXC-SFP-ZX-1G-CAN is one of thousands of fiber optic connectivity products available from L-com in-stock and ready to ship.
