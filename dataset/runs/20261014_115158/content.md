## **SO-QSFP-ER4** 

## QSFP+, 40GBase, CWDM 1270-1330nm, SM, DDM, 18.5dB, 40km 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-QSFP-ER4 is a QSFP+ (Quad Small Form-factor Pluggable Plus) transceiver for 40 Gbps applications such as inter- and intra-connect within and between data centers between switches, routers, storage equipment etc. 

The SO-QSFP-ER4 converts 4x 10Gbps flows into four CWDM channels in the 1300nm band up to 40 km over a SingleMode (SM) fiber.
