## **SO-SFP-100BASE-T & -T-I** 

SFP, 10/100Base-T, 100m, RJ45, C-temp & I-temp 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-SFP-100BASE-T is a transceiver with a high-performance integrated duplex data link for bidirectional communication over copper cable. It is specifically designed for high speed communication links that require 10- or 100-Megabit Ethernet over LAN cable. 

SO-SFP-100BASE-T is a solution for 10/100 Mbps Ethernet connections within racks and across adjacent racks where the interconnected equipment uses SFP interfaces instead of RJ45.
