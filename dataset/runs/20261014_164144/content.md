## **SO-XFP-ZR-Cxx** 

## XFP, 10G Multirate, CWDM, DDM, 24dB, 80km, 1470nm-1610nm (8ch) 

## OVERVIEW 

The SO-XFP-ZR-Cxx is a versatile CWDM transceiver supporting a wide range of traffic formats. The distance performance is in accordance with the industry ZR/ZW-standard, providing a bridgeable distance of up to 80km for 10GbE-LAN (10GBASE-ZR) and 10GbE-WAN (10GBASE-ZW) services. 

The transceiver is available in 8 CWDM wavelength versions, spanning from 1270nm to 1610nm in accordance with the G.694.2 standard. This transceiver provides digital diagnostic functions via a 2-wire serial interface as defined by the INF-8077i specification.
