**10Gb/s SFP+, Hot Pluggable Duplex LC, +3.3V, 1550nm Single Mode, 40 km, EML Optical Transceiver PSFP10-1541SF** 


## **DATASHEET** 

## **DESCRIPTION:** 

PeakOptical’s PSFP10-1541SF optical transceivers are designed for 10Gb/s serial optical interfaces for data communications with single mode fiber (SMF). The transceiver can support 1.25Gb/s to 11.1Gb/s. The transceiver designs are optimized for high performance and cost effective to supply customers the best solutions for datacom applications. 

## **FEATURES:** 

- Multi-Source Package with Duplex LC Connector 

- 10Gb/s serial optical interface 

- Up to 40km on 9/125um SMF 

## **APPLICATIONS:** 

      - 10G Base-ER/EW 

      - • 10G Fiber Channel 

      - Other Optical Links 

- Single +3.3V Power Supply 

- Hot-Pluggable 

- Compliant with Bellcore TA-NWT-000983 

- Compliant with Specifications for IEEE802.3Z 

- Compliant with SFP+ MSA 

- Eye Safety Designed to Meet Laser Class1, Compliant with IEC60825 

- Monitoring Interface Compliant with SFF-8472, Real time monitoring of: 

   - Transmitter optical power 

   - Receiver optical power 

   - Laser bias current 

   - Temperature 

   - Supply voltage 

- RoHS Compliant Products
